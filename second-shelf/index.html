How SECOND SHELF Works

A transparent marketplace platform. We connect sellers with book stalls and recycle scrap dealers without taking commissions or holding your funds in escrow.

📚

Used Books Lifecycle

  1. 1
    Submit Books & Photos: Enter title, author, condition (Like New, Good, Used, Damaged), original price, and 3 photos (front, back, inside).
  2. 2
    Estimated Payout Calculated: The system applies the admin-configured valuation percentage cap (30%, 20%, 15%).
  3. 3
    Stall Reviews & Sends Offer: Participating stalls make offers up to the condition cap. Seller accepts preferred stall.
  4. 4
    Handover & Physical Inspection: Seller hands over at stall or ships. Stall confirms receipt and inspects for missing pages or pirated copies.
  5. 5
    Final Price Confirmation: If condition matches, price is confirmed. If revised, stall provides written reason for seller approval.
  6. 6
    Direct Payment & Confirmation: Stall pays seller directly via UPI/Cash and logs the reference. Seller confirms receipt to complete.
♻️

Recycle Scrap Lifecycle

  1. 1
    Submit Request (≥ 10 kg): Pick paper category, enter estimated weight (must be 10 kg or more), area PIN code, and preferred pickup slot.
  2. 2
    Privacy-First Recycler Feed: Approved recycle scrap dealers see only the weight, type, and area/PIN code. Personal address & phone stay hidden!
  3. 3
    Dealer Proposes Rate per kg: Recycle scrap owner quotes a competitive rate/kg based on current market recycling prices.
  4. 4
    Seller Accepts Offer: Exact address and phone are unlocked for that specific dealer to coordinate doorstep pickup.
  5. 5
    Doorstep Weighing & Inspection: Paper is weighed on electronic scale. Actual weight and total amount confirmed.
  6. 6
    Two-Party Payment Confirmation: Dealer transfers via UPI/Cash and records reference. Seller confirms receipt to mark Completed.
